Our Story

A Heart for Honest Baking

“Flour, water, salt, and time. When done with patience, the simplest elements become pure joy.”

Founded as an independent neighborhood patisserie, Maison Dorée was born out of a deep reverence for French baking traditions and artisanal sourdough craft. We believe great bread cannot be rushed, and memorable cakes should taste even better than they look.

Every morning at 4:30 AM, our bakers begin kneading dough, rolling viennoiserie, and whipping silky ganaches. We source our dairy directly from local pasture-raised farms and prioritize natural ingredients in every creation.
